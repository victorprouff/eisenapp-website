Download EisenApp

Available for macOS, Windows and Linux.

macOS Apple Silicon .dmg macOS Intel .dmg Windows .exe Linux .AppImage

⚠ The app is not signed

On macOS: right-click the app → Open, or run xattr -cr "/Applications/EisenApp.app" in Terminal.

On Windows: click "More info" then "Run anyway" in the SmartScreen dialog.
